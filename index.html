TMH Market Research

An internal, read-only product research application being developed by The Midnight Hive. The application is intended to use approved platform APIs to organize public listing and shop information into internal research briefs for product planning.

Limited internal use · no public customer access

Read-only research

The research phase is designed to inspect approved public marketplace data. It does not place orders, message members, manipulate engagement, or modify marketplace content.

Official API first

For Etsy data, TMH intends to use Etsy's official Open API within the scope Etsy approves for the application. Screen scraping is not part of the intended API integration.

Internal access

The current application is for the developer and colleagues working on internal product research. It is not offered to the general public and does not charge users for access.

Application purpose

TMH Market Research is intended to help an internal POD research workflow evaluate market opportunities using data made available through approved APIs.

  • Search and organize active listing information.
  • Compare public listing attributes, prices, tags, shops, images, and available engagement signals.
  • Prepare internal research briefs and trend observations.
  • Preserve source provenance so research can be reviewed by a human operator.

Boundaries

  • No copying of seller artwork.
  • No member messaging or engagement manipulation.
  • No purchasing, cart, favorite, or order automation.
  • No listing creation, editing, or publishing during this research phase.
  • No use outside the application purpose approved by the relevant platform.

Access to Etsy's API and any automated analysis remains subject to Etsy's review, approval, applicable scopes, rate limits, and API Terms of Use.
