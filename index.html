TMH Market Research

TMH Market Research is an internal, read-only application being developed by The Midnight Hive to support product and market research. For Etsy, the application is intended to use the official Etsy Open API only within the access and use case Etsy approves.

Private internal tool · no public user accounts · no marketplace writes

Read-only by design

The research workflow analyzes permitted marketplace information for internal planning. It does not create or edit listings, place orders, send messages, favorite items, or alter marketplace content.

Official API integration

Etsy data will be requested through Etsy's official Open API. The application will not use screen scraping as a substitute for the Etsy API.

Limited internal access

The current application is used only by The Midnight Hive's developer and authorized internal collaborators. It is not a public consumer service and does not sell access to Etsy data.

What the application does

The purpose of TMH Market Research is to turn permitted marketplace data into reviewable internal research briefs for print-on-demand product planning.

  • Search and organize active marketplace listings and public shop information available to the approved app.
  • Compare permitted listing attributes such as titles, prices, tags, images, shops, and available public engagement signals.
  • Identify patterns and market opportunities for internal product research.
  • Keep source references so a human operator can review the evidence behind research conclusions.

What it does not do

  • No copying or republishing of seller artwork.
  • No automated purchasing, cart activity, favorites, messaging, reviews, or engagement manipulation.
  • No listing creation, editing, publishing, or other Etsy write operations in this research phase.
  • No collection of payment details or private buyer data for the current research purpose.
  • No use of Etsy data outside the purpose, permissions, rate limits, retention rules, and terms applicable to the approved application.

API credentials are kept private and are never published on this website or in public source code.
